# AgriConnect: Key Quantitative Findings & Statistical Insights

## 1. Top Quantitative Takeaways
1. **The 68.5% Agribusiness Chasm**:
   * **87.0% (80/92)** want to start an agribusiness.
   * **100% of farmers under 45 years** (64/64) are willing to diversify.
   * Only **18.5% (17/92)** currently do so.
   * **100% of diversified farmers (17/17)** achieved net income growth.
2. **The Knowledge Void as Core Bottleneck**:
   * **56.5% (52/92)** cite "Lack of Awareness & Training" as the direct blocker stopping them from starting.
   * **51.1% (47/92)** explicitly request "Subsidy & Training" as their primary need.
3. **Smallholder Economic Fragility**:
   * **76.1% (70/92)** own <5 acres (Mean: 4.12 acres).
   * **29.3% (27/92)** cite "Money" (working capital deficit) as their #1 structural headache.
4. **Institutional Vacuum**:
   * **98.9% (91/92)** have received zero government or extension assistance.
   * **57.1%** of tech discovery occurs via YouTube and WhatsApp.
5. **High Non-Literacy Cohort**:
   * **38.0% (35/92)** are illiterate, mandating 100% voice and visual UX.
