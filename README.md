# AgriConnect: Farmer Marketplace & Advisory Strategy
> **An Evidence-Based Product Management Case Study & Strategy Portfolio**  
> *Developed from Primary Field Discovery with 92 Farmers across 7 Villages in Madhya-Pradesh India*

---

## Executive Summary
**AgriConnect** is a mobile-first agricultural micro-enterprise incubation platform and marketplace designed for India's 120+ million small and marginal farmers (holding <5 acres). Built upon rigorous primary field research, AgriConnect bridges the **68.5 percentage point drop-off between agribusiness intent (87.0%) and execution (18.5%)** by delivering zero-friction vernacular blueprints, certified <Rs. 3,500 starter input kits, and guaranteed pre-harvest local B2B buyer off-take.
