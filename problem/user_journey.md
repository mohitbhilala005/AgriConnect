## Stage-by-Stage Journey Friction Breakdown

### 1. Trigger (Stagnation & Debt Cycle)
* **User Context**: Ramesh (1.5 ac) earns barely Rs. 35,000 net after 5 months of wheat harvest. Input costs have doubled.
* **Emotional State**: Frustrated, anxious about family expenses.

### 2. Search (Information Overload & Disconnect)
* **User Action**: Searches "mushroom farming" on YouTube via 4G mobile.
* **Friction**: Videos show massive commercial setups costing Rs. 5 Lakhs; technical terms in English/Hindi are confusing.

### 3. Evaluation (Capital Anxiety & Institutional Vacuum)
* **User Action**: Asks local input dealer for mushroom spawn or polyhouse kits.
* **Friction**: Dealer does not stock specialty spawn; 98.9% have zero access to Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) extension officers.

### 4. Decision (The 68.5% Abandonment Point)
* **User Action**: Considers purchasing expensive uncertified spawn from distant city.
* **Friction**: **68.5% drop out here** due to fear of losing Rs. 5,000+ savings and lack of step-by-step guidance.

### 5. Action (High-Risk Unassisted Trial)
* **User Action**: The few who proceed (18.5%) attempt cultivation without climate-controlled SOPs.
* **Friction**: High mold/contamination rates (>25%) due to improper humidity or sterilization.

### 6. Outcome (Post-Harvest Distress Sale)
* **User Action**: Harvests fresh perishable produce.
* **Friction**: No local buyer connection; forced to sell to local middleman at 50% discount or let crop spoil.
