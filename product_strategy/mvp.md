# AgriConnect: MVP Scope & MoSCoW Prioritization

## 1. MVP Objective
To validate the closed-loop incubation flywheel with **500 smallholder farmers across 10 villages** in Central India, focusing specifically on **Oyster Mushroom Cultivation** as the pilot micro-enterprise.

---

## 2. MoSCoW Prioritization Framework

### Must Have (P0 - MVP Release)
1. **Vernacular Voice Discovery & Dynamic ROI Estimator**: Interactive voice-guided space and budget calculator that demonstrates transparent net profit formulas in Hindi and Malvi.
2. **Day-by-Day Visual SOP Task Checklist**: 45-day sequential task calendar with daily voice instructions, offline video caching, and completion checkboxes.
3. **1-Click Certified Starter Kit (COD)**: Bundled incubation kit (<Rs. 3,500) containing certified spawn, PP bags, formalin, sprayers, and temperature guide delivered with Cash-on-Delivery.
4. **Pre-Harvest Local B2B Buyer Matchmaking**: Automated Day (N-7) buyer broadcast connecting farmers directly to verified local restaurant/wholesaler buyers at a guaranteed floor price.
5. **100% Vernacular Voice Navigation**: Full accessibility layer supporting audio narration on every screen and voice input for illiterate farmers (38.0%).

### Should Have (P1 - Phase 2)
1. **Hyperlocal Peer Video Proof Feed**: 60-second video testimonials from nearby village farmers demonstrating their harvest and earnings.
2. **Automated Weather & Heat Stress Alerts**: Dynamic weather override that adjusts daily water misting tasks during sudden heatwaves.
3. **Photo Contamination Scanner (Agronomist SOS)**: Quick photo capture for suspected mold/contamination with async agronomist callback within 3 hours.

### Could Have (P2 - Phase 3)
1. **Micro-Financing / Pay-After-Harvest Split**: 50-50 deferred payment structure integrated with rural NBFCs.
2. **Multi-Mandi Real-Time Arbitrage Tracker**: Live price comparisons across regional wholesale mandis for semi-medium commercial farmers.

### Won't Have (Explicit Non-Goals for MVP)
* No open commodity crop trading (Wheat, Soyabean).
* No generic chemical pesticide e-commerce storefront.
* No expensive IoT sensor hardware integrations.
