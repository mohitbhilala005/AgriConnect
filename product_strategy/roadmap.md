## 2. Phase-by-Phase Release Specification

### Phase 1: MVP â€” The Core Incubation & Off-take Engine (Q4 2026)
* **Theme**: "From Zero to First Harvest in 45 Days."
* **Target Milestone**: 500 smallholder farmers across 10 village clusters completing their first mushroom cycle.
* **Key Features**:
  1. *Voice-First Dialect UI (Hindi/Malvi)*: Zero-text accessibility for non-literate farmers (38.0% of sample).
  2. *Venture Discovery & ROI Estimator*: Space & budget calculation engine.
  3. *Day-by-Day Visual SOP Task Calendar*: Time-series daily checklists with 45-second video demos.
  4. *Curated Starter Kit Ordering (<â‚¹3,500 COD)*: Direct village delivery of certified spawn & supplies.
  5. *Pre-Harvest Local B2B Buyer Matching*: Small-batch off-take matching with local restaurants and traders.
* **Success Gate to Phase 2**: >80% harvest completion rate; >â‚¹8,000 average net farmer earnings per cycle.

### Phase 2: Optimization & Risk Mitigation (Q1 2027)
* **Theme**: "De-Risking Cultivation & Social Trust."
* **Target Milestone**: Scale to 3,000 farmers; expand from Oyster Mushroom into Backyard Poultry & Polyhouse Greens.
* **Key Features**:
  1. *Photo Contamination Diagnostic Scanner*: Instant photo upload for fungal mold triage with agronomist voice callback.
  2. *Hyperlocal Peer Video Proof Feed*: Geo-tagged video stories from neighboring villages showing real harvest earnings.
  3. *Dynamic Weather & Climate Stress Sync*: Automated push alerts adjusting watering SOPs during temperature spikes.
* **Success Gate to Phase 3**: >70% repeat cycle retention; <5% crop contamination loss rate.

### Phase 3: Scale & Ecosystem Expansion (Q2â€“Q3 2027)
* **Theme**: "Financial Inclusion & Collective Scale."
* **Target Milestone**: 15,000 active farmers across 3 agro-climatic zones; institutional enterprise buyer onboarding.
* **Key Features**:
  1. *Micro-Financing (Pay-After-Harvest 50/50 Split)*: NBFC-backed starter kit financing with automatic harvest deduction.
  2. *Group Produce Pooling & Shared Logistics*: Aggregating 10 farmers' daily harvest into single tempo pickups for urban mandi dispatch.
  3. *Multi-Mandi Real-Time Arbitrage Engine*: Live price intelligence board for commercial diversifiers (Virendra).
