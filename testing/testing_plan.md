# AgriConnect: Usability Testing Protocol & Moderator Guide

## 1. Testing Objectives
1. Evaluate whether rural smallholder farmers can independently discover and calculate ROI for Oyster Mushroom within 90 seconds.
2. Measure task completion rates and cognitive friction across literate and non-literate cohorts (38% non-literate).
3. Validate System Usability Scale (SUS) score target (Target: >=85.0).

## 3. The 7 Usability Evaluation Tasks
1. **Task 1 (Discovery)**: Open the app and find the Oyster Mushroom blueprint using voice search.
2. **Task 2 (ROI Calculation)**: Set available space to 100 sq.ft and interpret net cash-in-hand profit.
3. **Task 3 (Ordering)**: Complete a 1-Click Starter Kit order using Cash-on-Delivery.
4. **Task 4 (SOP Video)**: Open Day 14 task and watch the slitting video in offline mode.
5. **Task 5 (Task Completion)**: Mark Day 14 task as completed and check the progress ring.
6. **Task 6 (SOS Agronomist)**: Trigger the Emergency Crop Doctor photo scan from the task view.
7. **Task 7 (Buyer Connection)**: Review B2B buyer offers and confirm a 40 kg pickup booking.
