# AgriConnect: Primary Field Research Summary

## 1. Study Overview
* **Sample Size (N)**: 92 verified farmers
* **Geographical Scope**: 7 villages across Madhya Pradesh (Chopna: 43, Mau: 18, Lalpur: 15, Lakadiya: 5, Tajpura: 5, Jalalpura: 4, Manakpura: 2)
* **Parameters Evaluated**: 28 discrete agro-economic attributes
* **Data Sanitization**: PII scrubbed, anonymized dataset indexed as F001-F092

## 2. Core Demographic & Farm Findings
* **Smallholder Dominance**: 76.1% (70/92) hold < 5 acres (Mean: 4.12 acres; 20.7% Marginal <2.5 ac, 55.4% Small 2.5-5 ac).
* **Demographics**: Mean age 43.3 yrs (58.7% aged 30-45); 38.0% illiterate, 62.0% literate. Mean farming experience: 26.1 yrs.
* **Cropping Pattern**: 95.7% practice mixed cropping. Dominant staples: Wheat (100%), Soyabean (93.5%), Corn (85.9%), Gram (54.3%), Green Gram (43.5%).
* **Water Vulnerability**: 64.1% rely solely on open wells, subject to acute dry-season depletion.

## 3. High-Value Discovery Findings
* **The Agribusiness Intent Chasm**: 87.0% (80/92) express strong willingness to diversify into agribusiness (100% of farmers <45 yrs), but only 18.5% (17/92) actively do so.
* **100% Income Success**: 100% (17/17) of active agribusiness practitioners reported positive net household income expansion.
* **Primary Adoption Blocker**: 56.5% (52/92) cite "Lack of Awareness & Training" as the single biggest barrier stopping them.
* **Capital Squeeze**: 29.3% cite working capital / money as their #1 structural farm distress factor.
* **Institutional Vacuum**: 98.9% (91/92) have received zero government scheme assistance or agricultural extension aid.
* **Tech Discovery Channel**: 57.1% of technology discovery occurs via YouTube and WhatsApp.
