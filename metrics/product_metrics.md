# AgriConnect: Product Metrics, AARRR Funnel & Operational Guardrails

## 1. North Star Metric (NSM)

$$\text{North Star Metric} = \textbf{Net Additional Monthly Income Generated per Active Farmer (Target: } \ge \text{Rs. 8,500 / month)}$$

### Why This Metric?
1. **Direct Value Realization**: A farmer only earns net profit if every component of the product succeeds: selecting the right blueprint, receiving certified inputs on time, executing daily SOPs without mold contamination, and completing a B2B off-take sale.
2. **Anti-Vanity**: Measures real rural livelihood expansion rather than superficial metrics like app downloads or page views.
3. **Aligned Incentives**: Directly bridges the 68.5% intention-to-action chasm identified in our 92-farmer field discovery.

## 3. Operational Guardrail Metrics (Risk Protection)

| Guardrail Metric | Threshold Ceiling | Justification & Risk Mitigation |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Crop Contamination Failure Rate** | **< 6.0%** | Protects smallholder savings from bad spawn or sterilization errors. |
| **B2B Buyer Default Rate** | **< 2.0%** | Platform guarantees payment escrow; protects farmers from market default. |
| **Offline Sync Failure Rate** | **< 0.5%** | Ensures zero task data loss in zero-connectivity village settings. |
| **Voice Recognition Error Rate** | **< 8.0%** | Prevents illiterate farmers from getting stuck during voice navigation. |
