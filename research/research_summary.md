## 3. High-Value Discovery Findings
1. **The Intention-to-Action Chasm (87.0% vs 18.5%)**:
   * 87.0% (80/92) want to start an agribusiness/diversification venture (100% of farmers under 45 yrs).
   * Only 18.5% (17/92) currently do so â€” a 68.5 percentage point drop-off.
   * **100% of those who diversified (17/17) saw income increase**.
   * Top desired ventures: Mushroom (20.0%), Vertical Farming/Polyhouse (18.8%), Poultry (11.2%), Vegetables (10.0%), Dairy (8.8%), Aeroponics (8.8%).
2. **Knowledge & Capability Deficit as Core Barrier**:
   * 56.5% (52/92) cite "Lack of Awareness & Training" as the direct reason stopping them.
   * 51.1% explicitly request "Subsidy & Training" support.
3. **Institutional Vacuum**:
   * 98.9% (91/92) received zero government or extension assistance.
   * Social media (57.1%) and peer farmers (35.7%) dominate informal info channels.
