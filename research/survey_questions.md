# AgriConnect: Primary Field Research Questionnaire (37 Parameter Guide)

Conducted across 7 villages (Chopna, Mau, Lalpur, Lakadiya, Tajpura, Jalalpura, Manakpura) in Central India.

## Section A: Demographic & Household Context
1. **Farmer ID** (Unique Identifier F001-F092)
2. **Farmer Name** (Isolated for Privacy / PII scrubbing)
3. **Age** (Years)
4. **Village / Gram Panchayat**
5. **Education Level** (Illiterate, Primary, Middle School, High School, Matriculation, Graduate)
6. **Total Farming Experience** (Years)
7. **Family Size & Active Farm Labor Count**

## Section B: Landholding & Agro-Climatic Baseline
8. **Total Landholding** (Acres)
9. **Farming Type** (Mixed Cropping, Double Cropping, Single Cropping)
10. **Main Crops Cultivated** (Wheat, Soyabean, Corn, Gram, Green Gram, Coriander, Mustard, Vegetables, Sorghum)
11. **Primary Irrigation Source** (Open Well, Well & River, River, Tube Well, Canal)
12. **Annual Crop Production Volume** (Quintals)
13. **Average Annual Input Spend** (Fertilizer, Seeds, Diesel, Pesticides)

## Section C: Modern Technology Awareness & Adoption Economics
14. **Awareness of Modern Agricultural Technologies** (Yes / No)
15. **Type of Technologies Known** (Sprinkler, Drip, Solar Pumps, Aeroponics, Hydroponics, Tractor)
16. **Primary Source of Agricultural & Tech Information** (Social Media, Peer Farmers, TV, Agro-Dealers, Govt Extension)
17. **Current Usage of Modern Technology** (Yes / No)
18. **Type of Modern Technology Used on Farm**
19. **Perceived Affordability of Modern Agri-Tech** (Yes / No)
20. **Observed Production Increase After Tech Adoption** (Yes / No)
21. **Observed Profit Increase Percentage** (0%, 5%, 10%, 15%)

## Section D: Agribusiness Participation & Latent Entrepreneurial Demand
22. **Current Involvement in Agribusiness / Secondary Enterprise** (Yes / No)
23. **Current Agribusiness Type** (Vegetable Farming, Goat Farming, Dairy, Poultry)
24. **Did Agribusiness Activity Increase Net Household Income?** (Yes / No)
25. **Willingness to Start a New Agribusiness / Diversified Venture** (Yes / No)
26. **Preferred Type of Agribusiness** (Mushroom, Vertical Farming, Poultry, Vegetables, Dairy, Aeroponics, Value Addition, Goat Farming, Hydroponics)
27. **Maximum Investable Capital for Secondary Enterprise** (<Rs. 5,000, Rs. 5k-10k, >Rs. 10k)
28. **Available Indoor / Non-Crop Space for Micro-Venture** (sq.ft)

## Section E: Pain Points, Structural Bottlenecks & Required Support
29. **Primary Unprompted Farming Challenge / Major Problem** (Money, Low Yield, Water Shortage, Aged Labor, Small Land, Market Support, Electricity, Flood, Transport)
30. **Secondary Crop Hazards** (Wild Animal Damage, Pest Attacks, Rain Delays)
31. **Primary Reason for Not Adopting New Tech / Agribusiness** (Lack of Awareness, Lack of Training & Resources, Money/Capital, Perceived Risk, No Need)
32. **Historical Receipt of Government Scheme / Extension Assistance** (Yes / No)
33. **Specific Type of Support Needed by Farmer** (Subsidy & Training, Market Support, Pure Training, Pure Subsidy, Resources)
34. **Preferred Training Delivery Format** (Short Mobile Videos, Voice Calls, Physical Demonstrations)
35. **Willingness to Sell Produce to Digital/B2B Pre-Harvest Buyers** (Yes / No)
36. **Primary Smartphone Ownership in Household** (Self Android 4G, Shared Family, Keypad Phone)
37. **Daily Mobile Internet & App Usage Habits** (YouTube, WhatsApp, Voice Calls)
