## 2. Deep Dive on Incumbents

### A. DeHaat
* **Strengths**: Extensive physical network of 10,000+ DeHaat franchise centers; robust institutional funding; end-to-end staple procurement.
* **Weaknesses**: Heavy asset footprint; focused on maximizing bulk chemical fertilizer and seed throughput for wheat/paddy; completely overlooks micro-space indoor diversification.
* **AgriConnect Strategic Moat**: We operate asset-light software incubation, transforming non-crop domestic space into Rs. 10,000/mo cashflow units without competing with DeHaat on chemical retail.

### B. Plantix
* **Strengths**: World-class image recognition for 500+ plant diseases; 10M+ downloads.
* **Weaknesses**: Purely reactive utility; monetizes via pesticide ads; does not increase farmer income or establish market links.
* **AgriConnect Strategic Moat**: We focus on proactive value creation (high-margin enterprise launch) rather than reactive pest management for low-margin staples.

### C. AgroStar
* **Strengths**: Strong direct-to-farmer brand recall; high-touch agronomy advice via toll-free helpline.
* **Weaknesses**: Fundamentally an e-commerce retail store; incentive is to push input sales rather than ensure net household profit.
* **AgriConnect Strategic Moat**: Our North Star Metric is aligned with farmer net profit (>=Rs. 8,500/mo), not gross input merchandise volume.

### D. Ninjacart
* **Strengths**: World-class logistics speed; fast payment settlements for commercial vegetable farmers.
* **Weaknesses**: Enforces strict minimum volume thresholds (50kg-500kg daily), completely shutting out smallholders with 10kg mushroom harvests.
* **AgriConnect Strategic Moat**: We aggregate hyper-local micro-harvests at the village level, enabling smallholders to collectively fulfill urban B2B buyer orders.
