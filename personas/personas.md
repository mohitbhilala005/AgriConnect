### 1. Profile & Demographics

* **Education**: Illiterate (Illiterate cohort â€” 38.0% of survey)

### 3. Goals & Motivations
* **Primary Goal**: Protect household survival; eliminate seasonal debt from local money-lenders for fertilizer/seeds.
* **Motivation**: Wants a reliable supplemental livelihood (Goat farming, Backyard Poultry, or Mushroom) to pay for children's education.

### 4. Pain Points & Barriers
* **High Fear of Capital Loss**: Cannot read written manuals or technical instructions; terrified that adopting new methods will fail and wipe out savings.
* **Input-Cost Squeeze**: 29.3% cite working capital as primary distress; constantly trapped in credit cycles with local agro-dealers.
* **Water Scarcity**: Well water drops sharply after January, preventing third-season cash crops.

### 5. Tech Usage & Information Habits
* **Device**: Uses a shared family smartphone or basic keypad phone; relies on children to operate WhatsApp.
* **Information Channel**: Village tea-stall peer discussions, local word-of-mouth (35.7% in survey), voice messages.
* **Accessibility Need**: Completely unable to use text-heavy interfaces; requires 100% voice assistance, big visual icons, and vernacular audio.

### 6. AgriConnect Product Opportunity
* **Zero-Text / Voice-First UX**: Voice search in local dialect + visual pictorial checklists.
* **Low-Risk Ancillary Enterprises**: Low-capex models like Backyard Poultry (11.2% preference) and Goat farming (3.8% preference) that utilize crop residues and require zero indoor climate tech.
* **Peer Community Validation**: Audio testimonials from verified nearby farmers in neighboring villages who succeeded.
