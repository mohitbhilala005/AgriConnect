## 2. Core Problem Statement

> **"Small and marginal farmers (holding <5 acres) in rural India struggle to diversify into high-margin agribusinesses and modern agricultural practices because they lack structured, actionable training, practical know-how, and reliable market linkages, resulting in perpetual dependency on low-margin traditional crops, stagnant household incomes, and persistent vulnerability to poverty."**

---

## 3. The 4 Structural Breakdowns (Evidence-Based)
1. **The Intention-to-Action Chasm (87.0% vs. 18.5%)**: 80 out of 92 farmers desperately seek diversification, yet 63 abandon the path before launching due to execution paralysis.
2. **The Knowledge Void (56.5%)**: Farmers lack step-by-step, localized standard operating procedures (SOPs), relying on disconnected YouTube videos that fail to provide real-time guidance.
3. **Working Capital Anxiety (29.3%)**: Smallholders cannot risk Rs. 20,000+ on unverified commercial ventures; they need micro-capital entry kits (<Rs. 3,500).
4. **Perishable Off-take Risk**: High-margin crops (mushrooms, polyhouse vegetables) spoil quickly without pre-harvest local buyer commitments.
