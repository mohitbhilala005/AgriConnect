# AgriConnect: RICE Feature Prioritization Framework & Formulations

## 1. The RICE Scoring Methodology

$$\text{RICE Score} = \frac{\text{Reach} \times \text{Impact} \times \text{Confidence}}{\text{Effort (Person-Months)}}$$

* **Reach**: Number of active farmers impacted per 1,000 cohort over a 3-month cycle.
* **Impact**: Value added to user income & UX (3.0 = Massive, 2.0 = High, 1.0 = Medium, 0.5 = Low).
* **Confidence**: Statistical backing from our 92-farmer survey (1.0 = 100% high empirical certainty, 0.8 = 80% moderate certainty, 0.5 = 50% speculative).
* **Effort**: Engineering, design, and operations effort measured in Person-Months (PM).
