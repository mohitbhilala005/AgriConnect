## 2. Selected Core Problem Statement

> **"Small and marginal farmers (holding <5 acres) in rural India struggle to diversify into high-margin agribusinesses and modern agricultural practices because they lack structured, actionable training, practical know-how, and reliable market linkages, resulting in perpetual dependency on low-margin traditional crops, stagnant household incomes, and persistent vulnerability to poverty."**

---

## 3. Why This Problem Matters (Evidence-Backed)

1. **Massive Latent Demand with 100% Proven ROI**:
   * **87.0% of farmers (80/92)** want to start an agribusiness activity. Among farmers under 45, willingness is **100% (64/64)**.
   * **100% of farmers (17/17)** who successfully diversified reported higher household income.
2. **The 68.5% Intention-to-Action Drop-off**:
   * Only **18.5% (17/92)** are currently able to run an agribusiness, revealing a **68.5 percentage point conversion bottleneck**.
3. **The Root Cause is Information Asymmetry, Not Farmer Reluctance**:
   * **56.5% of respondents (52/92)** explicitly stated they have not adopted because of **Lack of Awareness and Training**.
   * Only 2.2% cited risk aversion, proving that smallholders are eager to transform if provided guided execution.
4. **Structural Smallholder Reality**:
   * 76.1% of farmers own less than 5 acres. Incremental yield gains on commodity staples (Wheat/Soybean) cannot overcome the structural income ceiling; diversification into high-density cash crops (Mushroom, Polyhouse Vegetables, Dairy, Poultry) is mathematically required to double farmer income.
